How we run it

Our marketplace playbook.

01

15-point PPC audit

Structure, search terms, bids, placements and SKU profitability reviewed. A written action plan in 5–7 days.

02

Architecture rebuild

Products grouped by margin and role, then rebuilt into Discovery, Qualification and Dominance campaigns.

03

Calculated bids

Every target gets a bid from Target ACOS × conversion rate × price, then adjusted by placement.

04

Weekly harvest & negate

Converting search terms move into exact match; terms that spend without selling are negated.

05

Portfolio decisions

Each SKU tiered Scale / Sustain / Reduce / Sunset, so budget follows profit.

What we report

Numbers that matter.

Targets are agreed in writing before launch, then reported every month in plain language, next to blended results across every channel you run.

ACOS

Ad spend ÷ ad sales

How efficient advertising is on its own, compared against each product's breakeven ACOS (its margin).

Ad efficiency

TACOS

Ad spend ÷ total sales

Shows whether ads are building organic sales or just buying them. Falling TACOS is the goal.

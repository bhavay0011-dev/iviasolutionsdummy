Meta, Google & beyond

How we measure success.

Platform dashboards each grade their own homework. On Meta and Google we report against three numbers that can't double-count, with targets agreed in writing before launch.

MER

Total revenue ÷ total spend

Marketing efficiency across every channel combined. It cannot double-count, which makes it the number budget decisions are made on.

Breakeven ROAS

1 ÷ margin

The floor every campaign is judged against. With a 40% contribution margin, anything under 2.5× is losing money on the first order.

New-customer CAC

Spend ÷ new customers

What each genuinely new customer costs, and how long their repeat purchases take to pay it back.

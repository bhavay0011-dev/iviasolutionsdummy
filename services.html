Marketing Mix Modelling (MMM)

Budget scienceIncrementalityResponse curvesGeo-lift tests

Platform reports tell you what each channel claims. MMM tells you what it actually adds. We model your sales history against every channel, price, promotion and season, then use the response curves to move budget to where the next rupee earns most, and confirm each move with an experiment.

Explore Marketing Mix Modelling →Explore Marketing Mix Modelling →
  • Data audit and one clean weekly dataset across channels
  • Bayesian MMM with carryover and diminishing returns per channel
  • Validation on held-out weeks and calibration with lift or geo tests
  • Response curves, marginal ROI and a budget optimiser
  • Scenario plans for bigger, smaller or reshaped budgets
  • Regular model refreshes as new data and test results arrive

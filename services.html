Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Demand creationProspectingLead generationCatalog ads

People on Instagram and Facebook are not searching for you; great ads introduce you to them. We build consolidated account structures that give Meta's algorithm enough signal to learn, let creative do the targeting, and test new concepts every week so performance does not fade.

  • Account audit and consolidation: fewer ad sets, each able to exit the learning phase
  • Broad, creative-led prospecting with a separate creative-testing campaign
  • Catalog and dynamic product ads for e-commerce
  • Lead-form and website lead campaigns, with qualified leads fed back from your CRM
  • Meta Pixel + Conversions API with event deduplication
  • Weekly optimisation and plain-language action log

Google Ads

Intent captureSearchShoppingPerformance MaxYouTube

  • Keyword and intent research with separate brand, non-brand and competitor campaigns
  • Negative keyword management and search-term reviews every week
  • Merchant Center feed optimisation: titles, GTINs, images, disapprovals

Amazon & Marketplace Ads

Marketplace growthSponsored ProductsSponsored BrandsDisplay

This is where we started. Every Amazon account begins with a 15-point audit, then a three-tier campaign architecture (Discovery, Qualification, Dominance) with bids calculated, not guessed: Target ACOS × CVR × Price = Target CPC. The same discipline extends to Flipkart and other marketplaces.

  • 15-point PPC audit with a written, prioritised action plan in 5–7 days
  • Three-tier keyword and campaign architecture per SKU
  • Weekly bid reviews, placement modifiers and negative keyword additions
  • Search-term harvesting from discovery into exact match
  • 60-day product launch playbook for new ASINs
  • SKU-level portfolio tiering: Scale / Sustain / Reduce / Sunset
  • Amazon India, US, UK and UAE, plus Flipkart and other marketplaces
